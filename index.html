How it works

Three steps, every one of them watched.

Booking a ride is simple. What's different is everything running quietly underneath it.

01

Set your route

Enter pickup and drop-off. We show you your driver's name, photo, plate, and safety rating before you confirm.

02

Share your trip

One tap sends a live map link to anyone you choose — they see your route and ETA update in real time, no app needed.

03

Arrive, confirmed

We check the trip ended at the right place. If a route drifts or stops moving, our safety line reaches out first.

Built-in safety

Safety you can feel in the small details.

Not a single feature — a set of defaults that are on by default, not buried in a settings menu.

●

Live trip tracking

Every ride is GPS-tracked from pickup to drop-off, visible to you and anyone you've shared it with.

✓

Verified women drivers

Every driver passes an ID check, address verification, and an in-person interview before their first trip.

!

One-tap emergency line

A direct line to our safety team, staffed around the clock, reachable from inside any active trip.

★

Two-way ratings

Drivers rate passengers too, so trust runs in both directions, not just one.

▢

Route-deviation alerts

If a trip strays far from its expected path or stalls unexpectedly, we flag it and reach out.

◐

Night-ride mode

After dark, trips default to extra check-ins and a shorter response window from our safety team.

⌁

In-app trip recording

Audio recording can be started from the trip screen at any point, stored securely if it's ever needed.

♀

Women-only option

Choose to be matched exclusively with women drivers and riders on every trip, every time.

●  The driver side

✓

Government ID, proof of address, and a face match before approval.

✓

A real conversation with our team — not just a form submitted online.

✓

Vehicle inspection

Roadworthiness, insurance, and registration checked before the car goes live.

✓

Ongoing rating review

Driver scores are reviewed continuously, not just at sign-up.
