● Why we exist

Ride-hailing solved convenience. It never solved feeling safe.

For a lot of women, the question isn't just "how do I get there", it's "who's driving, does anyone know where I am, and what happens if something feels wrong." SheSafeDrive started because that second question deserved a real answer, not an afterthought.

Most ride-hailing apps were built around one job: matching a rider to the nearest available car, as fast as possible. Safety features got added later, bolted onto a system that was never designed around them, an emergency button buried three menus deep, a "share trip" feature nobody remembers to use, driver checks that happen once and never again. For women riding alone, especially at night, that gap is the difference between a ride that feels fine and one that doesn't.

3 in 5 women report feeling unsafe taking a solo ride at night, regardless of platform
68% say they've cancelled or cut short a ride because something about it felt off
1 in 4 don't know how to reach in-app safety support when they actually need it
92% say they'd choose a women-only or safety-first option if it were available

Figures are illustrative estimates compiled from industry research and rider surveys, used here to frame the problem we're building for.

● Our mission

We build the ride-hailing experience we wish already existed: one where safety is the default, not a feature you have to go looking for.

Safety first, not safety-washed

Every feature we ship has to make a real trip safer — not just look reassuring in a screenshot.

Trust runs both ways

Drivers deserve respect and fair treatment as much as riders deserve protection. We design for both.

Built with the people who use it

Our safety features come from talking to riders and drivers directly, not guessing what they need.

● Our timeline

How we got here.

Still early days, but every step has been deliberate.

2023

The idea takes shape

After hearing the same story from rider after rider — cancelled trips, late-night anxiety, no clear way to reach help — the first concept for SheSafeDrive was sketched out.

2024

First rides in Lagos

SheSafeDrive launched with a small group of verified women drivers, live trip-sharing, and a direct safety line — testing the model where it mattered most.

2025

Night-ride mode & route alerts

Built and shipped automatic check-ins for late-night trips, plus route-deviation alerts that flag a trip the moment something looks off.

2026

Growing beyond Lagos

Expanding our driver network and safety operations into new cities, with the same checks and standards from day one — not a lighter version of them.
